## 🇨🇳 中文

把用户确认的影片设定、准确文字、字体系统、镜头、转场、可见特效和完整 15 秒配乐，编译成一条以片名落板收束的片头或概念预告。流程带三道确认闸门：先确认成片范围（单条 15 秒继续，超过 15 秒或多片段完整预告直接停下），再在同一次确认里问定四种主视觉载体、屏幕语言与准确文字，存在参考图时另设闸门单独确认参考图约束强度。四种主风格从固定入口选择：电影叙事、实拍动态图形合成、架空世界特效、编辑拼贴包装。片名、导演卡与 2–3 位角色姓名卡逐字核对，不影射现实明星、现有电影或受保护品牌；用户额外描述的美术风格会按制作机制就近映射，并生成仅服务本任务的临时风格总结，而不是新增入口。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 四类主视觉载体固定入口：电影叙事／实拍动态图形合成／架空世界特效／编辑拼贴包装
- 三道确认闸门：成片范围、主风格＋屏幕语言＋准确文字、参考图用途；未确认不写最终提示词、不启动生成
- 15 秒内完成多镜头、转场、可见特效与完整配乐，默认 16:9、2K、24fps、同步音乐与环境声、中英双语屏幕文字
- 文字内容三选一：基础电影卡司／基础卡司＋一句剧情钩子／用户自定义准确文字
- 参考图三种约束强度：参考人物与影调、严格参考原景与人物、自定义参考范围
- 无素材时可用原创虚构人物、世界、卡司与片名，但必须先进入文本方案让用户确认

## 🇬🇧 English

Compiles an approved film treatment, exact on-screen text, typography system, shots, transitions, visible VFX, and a complete 15-second score into one title sequence or concept teaser that resolves on the title reveal. Three gates structure the run: scope (a single 15-second piece proceeds, anything longer or multi-clip stops here), then one confirmation covering the four main visual vehicles, screen language, and exact text, then a separate gate for how tightly reference images constrain the result whenever any exist. The four fixed style entries are cinematic narrative, live-action motion-graphics composite, constructed-world VFX, and editorial collage packaging. Titles, director cards, and two to three cast name cards are verified word by word with no real stars, existing films, or protected brands implied; extra art styles the user describes are mapped to the nearest production mechanism through a task-local style summary instead of a new entry.

### ✨ What it does

- Four fixed style entries: cinematic narrative, live-action graphics composite, constructed-world VFX, and editorial collage packaging
- Three gates — scope, style plus screen language plus exact text, and reference-image usage — with no final prompt or generation before approval
- Multi-shot, transitions, visible VFX, and a full score inside 15 seconds at 16:9 / 2K / 24fps with synced music, ambience, and bilingual screen text
- Three text modes: base cast cards, base cast plus one story hook line, or user-supplied exact text
- Three reference-image strengths: character and grade, strict original set and character, or custom scope
- With no assets it invents original fictional people, worlds, cast, and title — approved as text first, never mirroring real stars or brands
